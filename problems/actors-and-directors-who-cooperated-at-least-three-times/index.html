1050. Actors and Directors Who Cooperated At Least Three Times

Intuition & Approach

To solve Actors and Directors Who Cooperated At Least Three Times efficiently, we can use standard algorithms and data structures. Explaining the logic, time complexity, and space complexity here will help you understand the solution better.

Code Solution

SQL
SELECT actor_id, director_id
FROM ActorDirector
GROUP BY 1, 2
HAVING COUNT(*) >= 3;
